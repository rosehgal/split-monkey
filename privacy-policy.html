Split Monkey privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Split Monkey stores your profile, people, groups, expenses, settlements, and captured transaction text in an encrypted vault on your iPhone. The app does not require an account and does not operate a cloud database or analytics service.

If you choose to share a group update, QR code, link, report, or message, the content you select leaves the app through the iOS share sheet, clipboard, or the app you choose. Other apps and recipients then handle that content under their own terms. Shortcuts automations are configured and run by iOS; Split Monkey receives only the text passed to its App Intent. Receipt images are processed on the device for text recognition. Apple Intelligence extraction, when selected and available, runs through Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework.

Optional review notifications contain no transaction amount, merchant, or message text. Notification settings can be changed in iPhone Settings.

The vault key is kept in the device Keychain and is not portable. The vault is excluded from iCloud backup. Deleting the app or losing the device can permanently remove your data. Export important group reports before changing devices or uninstalling.

Split Monkey does not sell or track your data. To request help or ask a privacy question, use the contact on the support page.
